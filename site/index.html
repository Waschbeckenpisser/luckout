What's it about

Luckout is a slot roguelike: you spin a 3×3 slot machine, and every win hits your opponent.

🎰

Reels instead of fists

Every win deals damage equal to its payout. You get only a few spins per fight, so every spin counts.

⏳

10 eras

From the Prehistoric Age to the Future. Each era has 12 fights, bosses in fights 4 and 8 and a champion in fight 12. After that, the Endless mode keeps going.

🃏

Relics & synergies

154 relics, 55 consumables and 15 curses. Combine them into a build that fits your run.

😈

Devil & Tax Grandpa

The Devil steals your bet. Tax Grandpa 👴 takes 30% of your winnings. You can outsmart them both.

The story

Your boxing career is in ruins. At the back of a dusty arcade stands a machine: LUCKOUT. You choose your own ring name.

Three sevens, and the machine opens a time portal. On the other side, a champion waits in every era, and none of them wants to let you leave.

Your goal: get back home and still win the title fight.

Play on your phone

Luckout runs in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it starts like an app.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open Luckout in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Luckout in Chrome.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose “Install app”.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Luckout is free and has no ads.

Do I have to sign up?

No. Just open the page and start playing.

Is my progress saved?

High scores, your name and unlocked levels stay local in your browser. A run in progress is not saved.

Is there an app?

An app is planned. Until then, you can add Luckout to your home screen as described above.

What data is collected?

None. There is no tracking. When the page loads, only the fonts are fetched from Google Fonts.
